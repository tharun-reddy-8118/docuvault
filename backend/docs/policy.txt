ACME Corp Employee Handbook - 2026 Edition

1. Working Hours & Remote Policy:
Standard hours are 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM EST. All employees have "Focus Fridays" where no internal meetings are permitted after 1:00 PM. Employees are allowed up to 30 days of "Work from Anywhere" globally per calendar year.

2. Equipment & Gear Reimbursement:
Each new employee receives a $1,500 one-time home office setup stipend. In addition, there is an annual "Ergonomic & Wellness" allowance of $600 that covers gym memberships, standing desk upgrades, or blue-light glasses.

3. Secret Project:
Project Falcon is the codename for our next-generation quantum-resistant encryption router, scheduled for release in Q4 2027 under lead architect Dr. Elena Vance.
